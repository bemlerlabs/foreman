## What is **not** proven (named honestly)

  no payment to it: a hire action requires an `aat_` token and escrow funding. The plan explicitly
  allows for G4 "third-party hire dialed back to offered+proven". The README and video may
  therefore say "live quoted offer", not "hired a third-party agent".

  and the adapter needs `wasmtime` + `ephemora_cell`. For the judge cold test (G6) this must
  either be shipped or built in the quickstart — otherwise the demo silently shows the mock
