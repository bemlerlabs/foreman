# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Foreman is built by people and agents in the open, and it is judged by people who did not ask to be
here. Contributors, reviewers and anyone running the demo agree to keep the discussion about the
work: the contract, the receipts, the numbers, the reproduction steps.

This project's whole premise is that claims must be checkable by someone who does not trust the
speaker. That applies to how we treat each other too — a disagreement is settled by pointing at an
artifact, not by rank or volume.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique the change, not the person. "This payout test does not cover the clamp" is welcome;
  commentary on who someone is does not belong in a review.
- Say what you actually observed. A claim about behaviour you did not run gets labelled as a guess.
- Assume a report is sincere until it is shown not to be, especially from someone who found a bug
  on a money path.
- Respect that contributions may be AI-assisted and still need a human who can answer for them.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, slurs, sexualised attention, doxxing, or any "just explaining how it really works"
  version of the above.
- Publishing someone's credentials, keys, account names or private messages, in an issue or in a
  screenshot. If you have one, rotate it first.
- Farming the demo: draining testnet escrow accounts, spamming bids on the shared demo job, or
  posting fabricated receipts as ours.
- Presenting an unverified number as measured. This project removes its own claims when they turn out
  to be wrong; we expect the same candour from others.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces: this repository, issues and pull requests, the demo board and its
accounts, and any hackathon channel where Foreman work is being discussed. Outside those spaces it
still applies when conduct there affects someone's ability to contribute here.

## Reporting

Open a private security advisory (<https://github.com/bemlerlabs/foreman/security/advisories/new>)
or write to a maintainer through the same page. Reports are read by the maintainers of this
repository only, kept confidential as far as the platform allows, and acted on within a week.
Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is itself a violation.

## Consequence

A first breach is usually a request to edit or withdraw the specific comment and a short cool-off.
Repeated or serious conduct — harassment, exposing someone's secrets, fabricating evidence — gets a
pull request closed without merge, contributions reverted where they touch the affected area, and a
temporary or permanent ban from the project spaces. Decisions and their reasons are recorded in the
thread that triggered them, minus anything that would identify the reporter.

## Attribution and limits

The expectations above follow the Contributor Covenant, whose canonical text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>; this page states them for this
repository instead of importing the full document and a contact address nobody reads. Enforcement
answers follow the same source's moderation guidance.
